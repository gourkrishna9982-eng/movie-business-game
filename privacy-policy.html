Privacy Policy

Movie Business Game

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Movie Business Game ("the Game") handles information when you use the Game.

1. Information We Use

The Game uses the email address you enter on the login screen to associate your game account and saved game progress with your account.

The Game also stores game progress data, such as the current game state, so that your progress can be saved and restored.

2. Local Storage

The Game may store your game progress in your browser's local storage. This local backup is used to help preserve your progress on that browser and device.

3. Server Cloud Save

When you are logged in, the Game can send your email address and saved game state to the Game's server so your progress can be saved and restored from the cloud.

4. Google Drive

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the Game requests access to Google's application-specific Drive storage (the appDataFolder). The Game uses this storage only for its cloud-save file.

The Game does not request general access to your personal Google Drive files. Connecting Google Drive is optional.

Google authentication and Drive access are handled by Google. You can review Google's privacy practices at Google's Privacy Policy .

5. Cookies and Tracking

The Game does not intentionally use advertising trackers or sell personal information. Its save features use browser storage and, when enabled, cloud services described above.

6. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the Game is used to provide login/account association, save game progress, restore game progress, and maintain the Game's save functionality.

7. Data Sharing

The Game does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by the services required to provide the save functionality, including Google when you voluntarily connect Google Drive.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information used by the Game's save functionality. However, no internet service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Choices

You can choose not to connect Google Drive. If you do connect it, you can manage or revoke the Game's Google access through your Google Account permissions.

10. Children's Privacy

The Game is not intended to knowingly collect more personal information from children than is necessary to provide its basic functionality.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Game's data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
